How to print on 4×6 paper
  1. Tap Print now (or Save 4×6 photo, then Share and Print from Photos).
  2. Choose your printer and set paper size to 4×6.
  3. Print, then cut down the middle line to get two strips.

Touch and hold the photo, then choose Add to Photos.
